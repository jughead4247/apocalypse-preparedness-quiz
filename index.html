About the Apocalypse Preparedness Quiz

What if the apocalypse started at this exact moment? This fictional preparedness quiz examines your current situation, practical abilities, physical capability, resources and adaptability.

You'll answer 30 questions based on what you actually have and what you can currently do. Your choices produce a preparedness score from 0 to 100%.

The quiz is designed around one simple idea: how prepared are you if everything changes right now?

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the quiz work?

You'll answer 30 questions about your current skills, resources, physical ability, medical preparedness, living situation and adaptability. Each answer contributes to your final preparedness score.

What is the maximum score?

Your final preparedness score ranges from 0% to 100%.

What does my score mean?

Your score represents your fictional level of preparedness based on the answers you provided. A higher score means you currently have more useful advantages for dealing with an apocalyptic scenario.

Is this a real survival test?

No. This quiz is fictional entertainment. It does not scientifically predict how someone would perform during an actual disaster or apocalypse.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. You can restart the quiz at any time and try different answers to see how your result changes.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. After completing the quiz, use the Challenge Your Friends button to share your preparedness result and see who is better prepared.
