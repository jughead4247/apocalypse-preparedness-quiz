About the Apocalypse Preparedness Quiz

The Apocalypse Preparedness Quiz is a fictional decision-making and preparedness assessment designed to answer one question: How prepared would you be if normal life suddenly disappeared?

Instead of focusing on a single type of disaster, this quiz looks at different areas that could affect your ability to cope with a major fictional emergency.

How It Works

The quiz contains 30 questions. Each question presents a situation or preparedness challenge and gives you several possible choices.

Your answers are used to calculate an overall preparedness percentage based on the choices you make throughout the test.

What Does the Quiz Test?

The questions cover several different areas of preparedness, including resources, practical skills, medical knowledge, physical ability, adaptability, decision-making, and your current environment.

This means that being prepared is not simply about having a large supply of food or equipment. Knowledge, planning, flexibility, and the ability to make decisions can also influence your fictional preparedness result.

Preparedness Score

Your final result is displayed as a percentage from 0% to 100%.

A higher score means that your answers demonstrated a greater level of preparedness within the fictional situations presented by the quiz.

The score is created specifically for this entertainment experience. It is not a scientifically validated measurement of someone's real-world preparedness.

There Isn't Always One Perfect Answer

Real emergencies can be unpredictable, and the fictional situations in this quiz are designed to make you think.

Some decisions may appear useful in one situation but create problems in another. The quiz therefore focuses on the overall pattern of decisions you make rather than treating every situation as a simple right-or-wrong question.

A Fictional Scenario

The apocalypse scenarios used in this quiz are fictional. They are designed for entertainment and interactive decision-making.

The quiz should not be considered emergency training or official disaster-preparedness guidance.

Take the Test Again

You can replay the quiz and make different choices to see how your preparedness result changes.

Try to think about what you would genuinely do rather than simply selecting the answer that appears to give the highest score.

Just for Fun

The Apocalypse Preparedness Quiz is an entertainment experience. It is not a professional emergency, medical, military, psychological, fitness, or survival assessment.
